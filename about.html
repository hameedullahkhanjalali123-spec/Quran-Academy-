Pedagogical Framework

Our Proven 4-Phase Quran Retention Framework

Read Full Methodology
01

Daily Sabaq (New Lesson)

1-on-1 articulation correction with real-time pointer tracking on digital Mus'haf screen.

02

Sabqi (Recent Revision)

Recitation of the last 4-8 lessons before concluding every class to cement retention.

03

Manzil (Cyclical Daur)

Systematic repetition of earlier completed Juz so the student never forgets past chapters.

04

Islamic Tarbiyah & Duas

5 dedicated minutes per session for daily Masnoon Duas, 6 Kalimas, and moral Islamic character.
