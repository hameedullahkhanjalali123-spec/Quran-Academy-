Step-by-Step Daily Routine

The 4-Pillar Lesson Structure

Every 30-minute 1-on-1 session is systematically structured into four distinct instructional phases:

Phase 1 (12 Mins)

Daily New Lesson (Sabaq)

The teacher recites first with screen highlighting. The student repeats line-by-line while articulation points (Makharij) and Harakaat are corrected in real time.

Phase 2 (8 Mins)

Recent Revision (Sabqi)

Before moving forward, the student recites the last 4 to 8 previous lessons. This eliminates short-term memory gaps and solidifies letter combinations.

Phase 3 (5 Mins)

Cumulative Daur (Manzil)

For Nazira and Hifz students, previous completed Juz or chapters are rotated systematically so that completed material is retained effortlessly for life.

Phase 4 (5 Mins)

Islamic Etiquette (Tarbiyah)

Each session concludes with 5 minutes dedicated to Masnoon Duas, 6 Kalimas, practical prayer method (Namaz), and loving character building.

Interactive Learning Environment

How Live 1-on-1 Classes Work Online

Online learning at Q Islamic Academy is not a passive lecture. It is a live, real-time two-way interaction conducted over Zoom or Google Meet with high-definition digital Mushaf and screen annotations.

Color-Coded Tajweed Mushaf: Letters with Ghunna, Ikhfa, and Qalqalah are highlighted with distinct colors on-screen so visual cues reinforce phonetics.
100% Dedicated Attention: Only one student is in the virtual room with the teacher. No waiting in lines or sharing class minutes with other students.
Monthly Parent Progress Reports: Detailed reports tracking attended hours, Sabaq advancement, revision retention score, and teacher feedback delivered via WhatsApp.
Strict Female Privacy: Sisters and girls study with qualified female scholars in private, secure, and respectful environments.
